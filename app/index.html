Roadmap to the Platinum

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 at roughly 200 hours, with A Long Road (100% completion) the hardest step. The story runs five chapters, about 60–80 hours. Nothing needs multiplayer, DLC or online play. Work the phases in order — each phase lists the trophies it covers.

Do this first: in Chapter 1, meditate at home the first time it is offered — Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy reported as potentially missable, and that first spot is gone after Chapter 1. And never turn on a cheat: cheats disable trophies and saving.

Missables

One trophy is reported as potentially missable. Everything else can be finished after the story.

MISSABLE Positive Mental Attitude Bronze

Official description: “Meditate at every spot across Leonida.” — 18 spots in 10 locations.

PowerPyx flags this as the only potentially missable trophy: the first meditation spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 and becomes permanently unavailable after that chapter. Meditate the first time it is offered, then find the other 17 spots as you explore. If you are past Chapter 1 and never meditated at home, that spot is gone — check the guide before starting a fresh run.

Everything else is safe

  • Story trophies (12) are automatic and cannot be missed — they unlock when their chapter or mission ends. (PowerPyx)
  • There is no mission replay — and no mission medals — so nothing hides behind a hidden objective. (PowerPyx)
  • Quest givers come back: some go temporarily unavailable during the story and return later, so a strand that vanishes is not lost. (PowerPyx)
  • Side content stays open after the story, and progress made during the story counts toward the 100% checklist. (PowerPyx)

Save smart

  • Keep a few manual saves before the big missions — the usual insurance while the game is a day old and the missable list is still settling. (General advice)
  • Never turn on a cheat: cheats disable trophies, and you cannot save once one is used. (PowerPyx, GameSpot, PC Gamer)
